# Add Kananga municipal profile page with officials and barangays

Cycle: 20260904-a03baa
Started: 2026-09-04T01:40:00.000Z

## Goal

Give BetterKananga its first municipal content: a /kananga profile page showing the 2025 elected officials, the 23 barangays with 2020 census populations, and short sections on the geothermal economy and the Kaanyag Festival, all fed by new JSON data files under src/data/kananga.

## Approach

Add src/data/kananga/officials.json (term, source, mayor, vice mayor, eight councilors with party and votes from the ABS-CBN Halalan 2025 results at 100 percent of election returns) and src/data/kananga/barangays.json (23 barangays with 2020 PSA census populations via PhilAtlas). Add src/pages/kananga/index.tsx rendering quick facts from the MUNICIPALITY config, the officials, a barangay table with a total row, and the economy and festival paragraphs with source links. Register the /kananga route in App.tsx, add a top level Kananga item to mainNavigation, and add a /kananga entry to seo-metadata.json.

Defaults picked without blocking, revisable: route lives at /kananga as a new top level page rather than replacing the home government section (additive, nothing breaks) or nesting under /government/local (that tree renders region directories, not one municipality). Councilors carry their ballot names because the sources at hand do not publish full legal names. Data sits in JSON under src/data/kananga following the repo convention for directory data.

Tests come first and fail before the data and page exist: a data test validating counts, sums and cross checks against MUNICIPALITY, and a page test asserting the heading, the mayor and the barangay rows.
