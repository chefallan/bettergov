# Rebrand all locale strings to BetterKananga

Cycle: 20260904-b848b4
Started: 2026-09-04T10:30:00.000Z

## Goal

Remove the BetterGov branding from the 12 non English locale files and add the navbar Kananga label missing outside English, guarded by a unit test that scans every locale for leftover brand tokens.

## Approach

Text level replacements keep the diff minimal and the unicode escapes intact. In each of the 12 non English common.json files: swap the BetterGov.ph and BetterGov tokens to BetterKananga, move the copyright year to 2026, and add the navbar.kananga label. Filipino, Cebuano and Waray get properly rewritten municipal hero subtitles since those are the languages spoken in Kananga; the other nine keep their translated sentences with only the brand token swapped, because inventing sentences in languages without a reviewer risks wrong copy on a government site (revisable when native speakers review). Update the Filipino hero assertion in the e2e spec that was left on BetterGov while the fil locale lagged.

The seo-metadata rebrand was drafted here, hit the 400 line diff gate together with the locales, and moved to its own follow up cycle per the gate remedy; its change sits in a git stash and its test case ships with that cycle.

The new test reads every locale common.json from disk and fails on any BetterGov token, so regressions cannot land silently. Red first: the test failed on all 12 locales before the replacements.
